# SymfonyOnline January 2026

Date: January 22 – 23, 2026
Location: Online conference days will broadcasted directly on live.symfony.com. Click on "My account" to join.

Join us for the twelfth edition of the international online Symfony conference. The entire conference will take place
online for 4 days in English. Here is the conference agenda:

* January 20-21: online workshop days
  We offer several workshops per day, you can choose one 2-day workshop or two 1-day workshop to create your own workshop combo.
  Workshops will take place each day from 09:00 a.m. to 05:00 p.m (CET)
  No replay will be provided because workshops are interactive experiences.

* January 22-23: online conference days
  We will organize 2 tracks during each conference day
  All speakers will answer to the questions in live as talks will be pre-recorded during SymfonyCon Amsterdam 2025
  Talks will be in English with English subtitles
  Replay will be available as soon as each talk starts so you can connect whenever you wish!

Online conference days will broadcasted directly on live.symfony.com. Click on "My account" to join the online conference!
We look forward to seeing you virtually!

## 5 reasons to come to the conference
* Meet in real-life the people from the Symfony community you’re working with / talking to online the rest of the year
* Network with the community
* Discover the latest news about Symfony in prime time
* Ask all the questions you have about Symfony/PHP to the Symfony Core Team
* Have fun in a inclusive and friendly environment in a bright city!

## Venue
The Beurs van Berlage is a bustling historical meeting place in the heart of Amsterdam, directly
opposite the central station, a short distance from highways and Schiphol Airport. The Beurs van
Berlage is extremely easy to reach, both by public transport and by private transport.
